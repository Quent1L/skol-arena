### ✨ New

#### Championships with live standings

Create a championship, register players and let them enter their own matches. Standings update with every result, and a provisional view shows where everyone would stand once pending results are confirmed.

#### Enter a match step by step

A guided form walks you through the players, the teams, the score and the date, with a layout designed for phones.

#### Results confirmed by the other side

A result entered by one side waits for the other to confirm it. If you disagree, contest it with a reason and the score you think is right. A result nobody answers is validated automatically after 72 hours.

#### Games and the ways a match can end

**For super admins** · Super admins describe each game played (a discipline), with instructions on how to enter the score and the possible outcomes, such as a regular win or a forfeit.

#### Game rules

**For tournament admins** · Write the rules of a game once and attach them to your competitions.

#### Player profiles and statistics

Every player has a profile with their results and statistics, and can edit their own details.

#### Notifications

Get notified when a match involving you is entered, confirmed or contested.

#### Invite-only sign-up

New players join with an invitation code. Instances connected to Keycloak can also sign in with a company account, and forgotten passwords can be reset by email.

#### Install it like an app

A banner offers to install Skol Arena on your phone or computer, straight from the browser.

#### Ready to self-host

**For self-hosters** · Skol Arena ships as a Docker image. The database is set up automatically at start-up, and the first super admin account is created for you.
