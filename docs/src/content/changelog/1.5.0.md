### ✨ New

#### Ranked seasons

Play ranked: every match moves your rating, and players climb rank tiers on the season leaderboard. Super admins create seasons and set how scores count; players join a season from its page.

#### Match history and filters

Browse every match of a competition and filter them by player or by result.

#### Points on match cards

See at a glance how many points a match earned.

#### Victory quality tiebreaker

**For tournament admins** · Break ties in the standings by how convincing the wins were, based on the outcome of each match.

### ⚡ Improved

#### Redesigned tournament and ranked pages

Tournament pages, ranked seasons and the bottom navigation bar on phones have been rebuilt to be easier to read.

- Richer tournament statistics
- Player links open their statistics for the tournament you came from
