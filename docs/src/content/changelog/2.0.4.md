### 🐛 Fixed

#### Sign-in is protected against repeated attempts

After too many attempts, sign-in pauses for a moment and tells you how long to wait, in your language.

#### Private competitions stay private

Competitions attached to an organisation are hidden from non-members everywhere: matches, teams, live updates.

#### Correct client addresses behind a proxy

**For self-hosters** · Declare your reverse proxies with `TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS` so limits and logs see the real visitor. `ENABLE_EMAIL_PASSWORD` is renamed `SHOW_EMAIL_PASSWORD_FORM`.

- Rank tiers and game rules can only be changed by super admins
