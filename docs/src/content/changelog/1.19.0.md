### ✨ New

#### Know which version you are on

After an update, the app tells you the version you just got.

### ⚡ Improved

#### Only important updates block the app

Most updates install quietly and apply on the next page change. Only updates that cannot wait ask you to update right away.

### 🐛 Fixed

- Ratings and statistics are up to date when you come back to a tournament
- Weekly climbers and drops update after each validated match
- Rank progress bars stay correct after a tier is deleted
- Leaving a page no longer shows error messages
- Clearing the comparison filters clears the results
