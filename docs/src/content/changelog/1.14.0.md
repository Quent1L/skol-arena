### ✨ New

#### Skol Arena in English

The whole app is available in English as well as French. Pick your language in the settings.

#### Compare two players

Put any two players side by side, with their direct head-to-head record, for one game or one tournament.

#### Badges and personalised messages

**For super admins** · Set up rules that award badges and show tailored messages after a match, based on its date, game, score and more. Badges are re-checked every night and taken back if no longer deserved. In beta.

#### Badge tab

Each tournament shows the badges that can be earned and who earned them.

#### Losing streaks

Leaderboards, profiles and statistics show losing streaks alongside winning ones.

### ⚡ Improved

#### One filter bar for player statistics

Filter by game, tournament and mode the same way on profiles and comparisons.

### 🐛 Fixed

- The league points bar matches the real size of each tier
