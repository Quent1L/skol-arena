### ✨ New

#### Choose how results are validated

**For tournament admins** · Each competition picks its own rule: validated automatically after a delay, confirmed by an opponent, approved by an admin, or validated immediately. Players with a long record of accepted results can skip the wait.

#### Contest a result after it was validated

For 7 days after a match is validated, a participant can still contest it. Whoever entered a result can cancel it within 48 hours.

#### How ranked ratings work

A card in the tournament information explains the starting rating, placement matches and how much a match is worth.

#### Tune ranked ratings per game

**For super admins** · Make an outcome weigh more or less on ratings, and say how teammates interact in a game (playing together, side by side, or sharing resources) so ratings are shared accordingly.

#### Best partner and nemesis

Player profiles have been redesigned and show who you win most with and who beats you most.

#### Encouragement after a ranked match

The rating animation comes with a short message matching how the match went.

### ⚡ Improved

#### The score margin counts in ranked

A big win moves ratings more than a narrow one.

#### Cancelling a ranked match updates every affected rating

The ratings of everyone who played after it are recalculated, and the recap labels those changes.

#### A clearer rating recap

It shows your teammates, the match date and whether recalculated matches changed your rating.

#### Ratings update faster in long seasons

The leaderboard shows when a recalculation is in progress.

#### Create a match from anywhere on a phone

A create button now sits in the middle of the bottom navigation bar.

#### Problems with a match are flagged as you fill it in

The match form checks the players as you add them instead of waiting for the next step.

#### Season form errors are listed field by field

**For super admins** · A season can also have no placement matches at all.

- Your ranked profile lives in the Stats tab
- Bracket matches keep their players locked
- Tournament mode shown in the player statistics filter

### 🐛 Fixed

- A rank down is no longer announced as a promotion
- The rating recap lists matches by the date they were played
- Password reset emails are sent again
- The app no longer reloads twice after an update
- **For self-hosters** · Server memory stays stable over time
