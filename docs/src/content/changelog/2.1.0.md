### ✨ New

#### Player photos

Add a photo to your profile from the settings: crop it and it shows next to your name across the app instead of your initials. Super admins, and owners of an organisation you belong to, can remove a photo that is not appropriate.

#### Let the app build the teams

When entering a match, split the players into two teams in one tap: at random, or, in ranked, as evenly as possible based on everyone's rating. Tap again for another line-up; when the current one is already the most balanced, the app says so.

#### Icons for billiards, table football, darts and more

**For super admins** · The icon picker now offers icons made for your disciplines: billiards, table football, darts, table tennis, pétanque, bowling, chess, cards, racket sports, video games and more. Search them in English or French ("babyfoot", "fléchettes"…). They work for disciplines, ranked tiers and badges, and every icon you already chose stays as it is.

#### Maintenance screen

**For super admins and self-hosters** · See your instance at a glance: app and database versions, database size, applied updates and the configuration in use (secrets only show whether they are set). When you change where player photos are stored, the same screen moves the existing photos to the new place.

#### Seasons that end on their own

**For super admins** · A ranked season can close by itself on its end date, or roll straight into the next one: the new season keeps the same settings, and every player is registered again so matches can start right away. New seasons are numbered from the ones actually played.

#### What's new: see what changed in each version

A new "What's new" entry in the menu under your avatar opens the list of changes, version by version, written for players and organisers. A small dot on your avatar tells you when there is something you have not read yet; it disappears once you open the list.

### ⚡ Improved

- Match dates fit on small phones

#### Search ignores accents

Typing "eloise" finds "Éloïse" wherever you look for a player: match entry, team picker, match filters, player comparison and the user list.

Self-hosters: the player comparison and the user list search on the server, which needs the PostgreSQL `unaccent` extension. Without it, those two searches still ignore upper and lower case but not accents. Match entry, the team picker and the match filters search in the app itself and ignore accents in every case. See [how to enable `unaccent`](/docs/deployment#accent-insensitive-search-unaccent).

#### Server logs are now easy to read by default

**For self-hosters** · The server now writes its logs as plain, readable lines instead of JSON, so `docker logs` is easy to follow. If a log collector reads your logs, set `LOG_FORMAT` to `json` or `logfmt` to keep the previous format.

### 🐛 Fixed

#### Ranked seasons and game rules are managed by super admins only

Moving a tournament to another organisation is also reserved to super admins, matching what the admin screens already showed.

#### Win rates agree everywhere

The same results now give the same win rate on profiles, head-to-heads, partners and tournament statistics: wins divided by matches played, with a tie counted as a draw only where the competition allows draws.

#### A single-use invitation code works only once

Signing up with an organisation's code also adds you to that organisation.

#### Organisation competitions stay private everywhere

Players outside an organisation can no longer see its ranked seasons, nor count its competitions in a player's statistics, comparison or badges.

#### Descriptions and notifications are better protected

Formatted descriptions are cleaned before being displayed, and notifications can only be delivered to genuine browser notification services.

#### Match results always go through validation

A result entered by someone outside the match needs both sides to confirm it, a scheduled match earns no points until a result is in, and you can no longer cancel a result entered by your opponent or a bracket match.

#### Team changes stay within their tournament

**For tournament admins** · Joining, leaving or deleting a team only works on teams of the tournament you manage, and a team can no longer be overfilled when several players join at once.

#### Trust is taken back when a result is withdrawn or disputed

Cancelling a result you validated yourself, or having a validated result disputed, removes the trust it earned you.
