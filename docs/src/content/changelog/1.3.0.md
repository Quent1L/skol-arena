### ✨ New

#### Kiosk accounts

**For super admins** · A shared screen, such as a tablet next to the table, can stay signed in with a kiosk account and enter matches for everyone. Its settings can be locked so players cannot change them.

#### Email sign-in on the installed app

**For super admins** · On an instance using company sign-in, tapping the Sign in button five times opens the email and password form, for accounts that have no company login.

### ⚡ Improved

- You stay signed in for 30 days
- Tablets and touch screens get the mobile layout

### 🐛 Fixed

- The back button on a match always leads somewhere sensible
