### ✨ New

#### Knockout brackets

**For tournament admins** · Run elimination brackets, with each round leading to the next.

#### Fixed teams

**For tournament admins** · A competition can be played by teams that stay the same all along, instead of line-ups picked match by match.

#### Rich descriptions

**For tournament admins** · Format a tournament description with headings, lists and emphasis.

#### Score limits, or no score at all

**For tournament admins** · Set a minimum and maximum score, or turn score entry off when only the winner matters.

#### Recalculate the points of a championship

**For tournament admins** · After changing the points scale, recompute what every match already awarded.

#### Offline page

When your connection drops, the app says so instead of failing silently.

### ⚡ Improved

#### Match limits no longer block a match outright

In a championship, a match can still be entered as long as one player is under the match limit. It is flagged on the match page and its points are adjusted.

#### Smoother navigation

Swipe between tabs on a phone, and pages show their content progressively instead of all at once.

### 🐛 Fixed

- Cancelled matches no longer count anywhere
