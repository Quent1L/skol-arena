### ✨ New

#### Organisations

**For super admins** · Group players into organisations. A competition attached to an organisation is only visible to its members.

#### Watch your rating change

After a ranked match, an animation shows your rating moving and announces a rank up or down. When several results arrive at once, a recap sums them up.

#### Provisional ranked leaderboard

See where everyone would stand if the matches still waiting for confirmation were validated. Swipe between the official and provisional views on a phone.

#### Sub-ranks and league points

Rank tiers are split into sub-ranks, and your progress inside a tier is shown in league points with a progress bar.

#### Context on ranked matches

Your match history labels notable results, such as beating a stronger opponent.

#### No more double bookings

A player can no longer be entered in two matches at the same time in a tournament.

### ⚡ Improved

#### Clearer brackets

Brackets are drawn with connectors between rounds, bigger match cards and round names that stay visible while you scroll.

#### Player avatars everywhere

Players appear with a coloured avatar on match cards, brackets and in the header.

#### Faster date entry

The date picker has a "Today" shortcut and accepts typed dates.

#### Ranked ratings weigh the outcome

A win counts more or less depending on how the match ended, and ranked matches show the rating change instead of points.

#### Display names follow simple rules

A display name needs at least 3 characters, including 2 letters or digits.

- **For tournament admins** · Correcting a match you played in confirms your own side

### 🐛 Fixed

- Two evenly matched players win and lose the same amount of rating
- Dates no longer shift by a day depending on your time zone
- The match date is saved when you create or edit a match
- Tournament statistics update as soon as a match is validated
