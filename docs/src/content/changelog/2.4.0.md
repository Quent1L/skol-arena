### ✨ New

#### See who already earned each badge

In a tournament's Badges tab, each badge now shows the faces of the players who already earned
it. Tap them to see the full list, how many times each player won it, and who earned it this
season.

### 🐛 Fixed

#### Rules keep their date conditions

**For super admins** · A rule with a condition on the match date now shows that date when you open it again, and
saving the rule keeps it as entered. In the rule simulator, the match date is now picked
from a calendar.

#### Rule statistics leave out badges already held

**For super admins** · On the rules overview, the daily chart now only counts badges players actually won, and the
latest firings no longer list a badge rule that matched someone who already had the badge. A
switch above the list shows them again when you need them.
