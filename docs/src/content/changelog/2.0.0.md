### ✨ New

#### Talk it out on the match page

Each match has a discussion thread. When a result is contested, both sides can explain and settle it themselves, and every step of the match is recorded there. Tournament admins are asked to step in only when a dispute is escalated.

#### Expected result before the match

In ranked, see how balanced a line-up is before entering the result.

#### Career peak

Your statistics show your best rating across every season, not just the current one.

#### Rule statistics

**For super admins** · See how often each badge or message rule fires and what became of it.

#### Rules based on how a match ended

**For super admins** · Award a badge or show a message for a win by forfeit, for example.

#### Archive games and outcomes

**For super admins** · Retire a game or an outcome without losing the meaning of past results. Deletion stays possible only for what was never played.

#### Apply game changes to running competitions

**For super admins** · A banner lists the running competitions that still use the old settings of a game, and lets you update them one by one.

#### API reference

**For self-hosters** · Each instance can serve a browsable reference of its API at `/api/docs`, turned on with `API_DOCS_ENABLED`.

#### Configurable ranked reporting window

**For self-hosters** · `RANKED_MATCH_MAX_AGE_HOURS` sets how long players have to enter a ranked match after playing it (48 hours by default).

- **For super admins** · Choose whether an outcome counts in ranked

### ⚡ Improved

#### A rebuilt match page

The winner is highlighted as soon as a result exists, and who has confirmed is shown next to each name.

#### Badges can be won every season

Badges are now season trophies: win them again next season. A badge can still be made a one-off.

#### Fairer team ratings

In team matches, the rating change is shared between teammates. Ratings of ongoing seasons were recalculated once.

#### Fix a competition after it started

**For tournament admins** · Names, descriptions and dates stay editable all along. Score settings can be changed until the first result, and a new points scale recalculates what was already awarded.

#### Finished competitions keep their rules

Changing a game no longer rewrites the results of competitions already played under it.

#### Clear all your notifications in one go

The list loads as you scroll, and old read notifications are removed after 90 days.

#### Ranking cards explain their order

Best teams and players show avatars, links and a bar reflecting how the ranking is computed.

#### Pages show their layout while loading

A background refresh is signalled instead of swapping the figures under your eyes.

#### Reworked rating animation

The progress bar fills tier by tier during a promotion, and Escape closes the card.

#### A new look

Skol Arena has a new logo and colours.

#### Rule priority explained

**For super admins** · Priority is hidden where it does nothing, and team rules can target the whole winning or losing side.

#### Contesting a validated match happens in the thread

Agreeing again withdraws your contest, and tournament admins are notified.

- The match form shows how much each outcome weighs in ranked
- Season dates match when the season really started and ended
