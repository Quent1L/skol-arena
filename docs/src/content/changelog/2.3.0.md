### ✨ New

#### See how all your automatic rules perform on one screen

**For super admins** · A new statistics screen, opened from the rules list, shows every message and badge rule at a
glance: how often they were triggered, how many messages players actually read, and which
active rules have never been triggered at all.

Below it, a list shows the latest rules triggered — who got which message or badge,
and for which match — and can be narrowed to one rule, one kind of rule or one outcome.

#### See your win rate in 1v1, 2v2 and every other format

Your ranked profile now shows your win rate for each format you have played — 1v1, 2v2,
3v3, and uneven matches like 2v1 — with your wins and losses in each. No need to make the
top five of the season statistics to know where you stand. It also appears on player
pages.

- See when a tournament starts and ends from its details

### ⚡ Improved

#### A smoother app, on a phone too

Every screen of the app has been rebuilt on new foundations. Things look the way they did,
but menus, lists and dialogs now behave the same everywhere and fit a phone. The help
bubbles next to statistics open with a tap, and choosing a custom match date offers a
calendar.

**For super admins**: the administration now works on a phone too, and a menu lists every
section, so moving from one to another no longer means going back to the dashboard.

#### One back button on phones, always in the same place

On a phone, every screen now goes back with the arrow next to the Skol logo at the top. It
also takes you from a tournament's participants, teams or badges back to the tournament,
and while you record a match it steps back through the form before leaving it.

#### Updates no longer interrupt you

Opening the app no longer makes you wait for an optional update to download: it opens
right away and installs the update in the background. Once the new version is in place,
a small "Update installed" message shows at the top.

The full-screen update message is kept for the rare updates you have to install before
carrying on.

- Championship standings now look like ranked leaderboards

### 🐛 Fixed

#### The live ranking now counts matches awaiting validation in placement progress

**For players** · In the "Provisional (Live)" view of a ranked season, a match not yet validated already
moved your MMR and your recent form, but not your placement progress: you could stay
"3/5 placement matches" after playing your fourth. It now counts, and finishing your
placements with such a match places you in the live ranking straight away.

#### Swiping the standings no longer switches to an empty provisional table

On a phone, in a championship where results do not need confirming, a swipe on the
standings used to switch to a provisional table that does not exist there. The standings
now stay put.

#### The app no longer stays stuck on its loading screen

Going back to a Skol Arena page after an update could leave the pulsing logo on screen
forever. The app now always opens a version that matches what is installed, and if it
still cannot start, it reloads by itself.

- Profile pictures now show in tournament brackets
- Some error messages showed a technical code instead of a sentence
