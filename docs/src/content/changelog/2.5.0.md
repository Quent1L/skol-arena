### ✨ New

#### See how close you are to a badge

**For players** · In a season's Badges tab, a badge that counts matches now shows a progress bar with your
count, like "7 / 10". Badges earned by doing something once, such as a win streak, show no bar.

#### Choose your language before signing in

The sign-in, sign-up and password reset pages now have a language picker. On your first
visit, the app also opens in your browser's language when it speaks it, and in English
otherwise.

#### Badges and messages can now count matches

**For super admins** · A new "Match counter" block in the rule editor counts the matches that meet the conditions
you put inside it. You can now create a badge for "win 10 matches as Gold", "5 wins against a
Diamond player" or "20 matches played on a weekend".

The counter covers the current season, or every season of the rule's discipline, for example
a badge for "100 wins, all seasons combined". Counting across seasons is for discipline rules,
and for badges it requires the "once only" setting.

### ⚡ Improved

#### Open a badge to read its full description

**For players** · In a season's Badges tab, tap a badge to see its full description and who has earned it, instead
of a description cut off after two lines. From a player's ranked profile, a new "All tournament
badges" button takes you to every badge the season offers.

#### Players who leave a competition move to the bottom of the ranking

You can now leave a championship or a ranked season while it is running. Your results stay,
but you move to an "Inactive" section at the bottom of the ranking, without a position, so the
players still competing move up. Rejoin and you take your place back. In a ranked season,
playing a match is enough to rejoin.

When an organiser removes a player, that player is listed as inactive the same way, and only
an organiser can add them back. Until then, a match that includes them cannot be recorded,
and the app says why.

### 🐛 Fixed

#### No more "badge lost" message for a badge you still have

**For players** · When a badge you earned in one season was taken back for another season, you were told you
had lost it even though it was still on your profile. You are now only told when the badge is
really gone.

#### Badges using "contains" or line-up conditions are no longer lost overnight

**For super admins** · The nightly badge check now reads these conditions the same way as when a match is played,
so it no longer takes away, or fails to give, badges built on them.

#### The MMR summary shows who you played with and against again

**For players** · When several results came in while you were on a ranked season's page, the summary of your
MMR changes showed no player pictures until you reloaded the page. Your teammates and
opponents now appear straight away.

- Texts no longer stay in French when the app is in English
