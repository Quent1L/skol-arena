### ✨ New

#### Fix a result you just entered, without cancelling the match

**For players** · Picked the wrong winner or typed the wrong score? If your result was approved straight away,
you can now correct it from the match page for 48 hours, or for up to a week while an
opponent disputes it. The line-up and the date stay as they were played: only the result changes.

The other players of the match get a notification, and the match discussion shows the old
and the new result. In ranked seasons, ranking points are worked out again, and players who
had already seen their points for that match see a "result corrected" line in their next
summary.

#### Organisers can correct or cancel any approved match

**For tournament admins** · Organisers can now correct the result of any approved match in their championship or ranked
season, or cancel it, at any time. Use it to settle a disputed result. The players of the
match are told, and rankings and ranking points are worked out again.

### 🐛 Fixed

#### Badges show the date you earned them

**For players** · A badge used to show the day it was handed out, which could be long after the match that
earned it: when a result was entered days later, or when a new badge was given to everyone
who had already earned it in a past season. Every badge, including the ones you already
have, now shows the date of the match that earned it.

#### A cancelled match no longer counts in your ranking points summary

**For players** · When one of your ranked matches was cancelled before you had seen its result, the points
summary still showed it as a match you had played, with its points won or lost. It is now
left out, and the summary only shows the matches that really count.
