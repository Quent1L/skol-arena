### ✨ New

#### Season Rewind

When a ranked season ends, every player gets a story-style recap of their season: best moments, rivals, streaks and final rank. It is promoted on the home page for two weeks and stays available from your menu.

#### A home page built around you

The competitions you play in come first, then the ones you could join, with past events tucked away. A competition finished less than a week ago stays up front.

#### Peak and average leaderboards

A finished season can also be ranked by highest and average rating.

#### Carry ratings into a new season

**For super admins** · Start a season from the ratings of a previous one, softened towards the average by a factor you choose. Players stay off the leaderboard until they play their first match.

### ⚡ Improved

#### Fair podiums

Tied players share the same rank instead of being split arbitrarily, and a tie is never cut in half.

#### One switcher for leaderboard views

Swipe between leaderboard views on a phone, or pick them from a sidebar on a computer. The view you chose stays in the address.

#### Flip one outcome card at a time

Each outcome card has its own button to switch between winners and losers.

#### Last activity instead of last login

**For super admins** · The user list shows when each player last used the app.

### 🐛 Fixed

#### You stay signed in as long as you keep using the app

Active players were signed out about 30 days after signing in.

- Signing out no longer bounces you back into the app
- The statistics tab you were on is kept when you come back
- Top 3 lists no longer show a fourth player after a tie
