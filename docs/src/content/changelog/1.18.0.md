### ✨ New

#### User management

**For super admins** · A screen lists every account with its activity: sign-up, last login, matches and tournaments. Edit an account, reset its password, manage its organisations, deactivate, archive or delete it. Archiving removes the account but keeps its match history; a returning player can get it back.

#### Peak rating and weekly progress

Ranked profiles show your best rating and this week's gain, and tournament statistics list the week's biggest climbers and drops.

#### Filter the rating chart

Show only the last 25, 50 or 100 matches.

#### Create a password for a company account

Players who signed up with a company account can add a password from the settings.

### ⚡ Improved

#### Fairer rankings in statistics

Best partners, nemeses and outcome leaders take the number of matches into account, so one lucky win no longer tops the list. Each outcome shows both the most frequent and the most efficient players.

#### Clearer outcome breakdown

How often an outcome happens and who wins it are shown separately.

#### Shorter streak lists

Streak cards show the top 3, with a button to see more.

- Sign-in errors are translated

### 🐛 Fixed

- A renamed player shows their new name everywhere straight away
- Push notifications show the Skol Arena icon
- Icons no longer turn into empty squares after an update
- **For super admins** · A password reset email that cannot be sent is reported
