### ✨ New

#### Send an announcement to your players

**For tournament admins and super admins** · From a tournament or ranked season's menu, under "Announcements", write to all its players: new badges, a rule change, a reminder. Super admins can write to every player of the app.

It arrives as a notification, a pop-up shown once on their next visit, or both, with an optional end date for the pop-up. Closing the pop-up marks the notification as read.

#### Choose your own invitation codes, with no usage limit

**For super admins** · When creating an invitation code, you can now type the code yourself, like `club2026`,
instead of getting a generated one. A code can also have unlimited uses: handy for a link
or a QR code on the club's notice board. Codes are no longer case-sensitive when players
type them.

#### Try Skol Arena with ready-made demo data

**For self-hosters** · Start a new instance with `INIT_DEMO_DATA=true` to get a pool club a few months in: sixteen players, leagues, a ranked season, a cup, teams and about 400 matches, some pending, one disputed, votes already cast. The login page shows the admin's credentials with a one-click sign-in. A `docker-compose.demo.yml` is ready in the repository; see [Demo data](/docs/deployment#demo-data).

#### Fair-play and MVP votes

Organisers can switch on two optional votes for a tournament or ranked season. After a match, players give their opponents a 👍 for fair-play — or a 👎 with a reason, from the match page — and, in team matches, pick the MVP, which spectators can vote for too. Votes stay open 48 hours and never affect MMR or standings.

A prompt right after the match offers the vote in two taps. The stats tab ranks fair-play and MVP over 30 days (positive figures only), your own figures sit privately in "My profile", and ranked seasons show the fair-play player and MVP of the week. Organisers get a fair-play page to spot repeated issues, and the season recap gains a fair-play and MVP card.

#### Open sign-up to everyone

**For self-hosters** · An instance can now let anyone create an account, without an invitation code. The code
field stays on the sign-up screen as an option, so a code that adds players to a group
still works. See the environment variables page to switch it on.

#### Choose whether you are pre-selected when entering a match

A "Pre-select me" switch in Settings adds you to the players when you enter a match. It is on by default, organisers included; turn it off if you mostly enter other people's matches.

#### Sign in through any single sign-on provider, several at once

**For self-hosters** · Single sign-on is no longer limited to Keycloak: Authentik, Zitadel, Dex or any other
OpenID Connect provider works, and you can offer several side by side, each with its own
button on the login screen. Signing out of Skol Arena now also signs out of the provider
when it supports it. An existing Keycloak setup keeps working as it is, nothing to change on
the Keycloak side. See the environment variables page to add a provider.

#### See how a tournament is played

A "Format" card on the info tab explains in plain words how the tournament runs: competition type, match format, draws and scores, points, match limits, result validation and votes. Ranked seasons explain MMR there too. The written rules keep their own card, now called "Rulebook".

### ⚡ Improved

#### Going back to a tournament is instant

Back from a match or a player's stats, the tournament shows straight away, on the same tab and at the same spot, and still up to date.

#### Clearer player count when entering a match

The tournament's format now shows under "Participants" ("1 vs 1 · 2 players"), and a player too many — or an unbalanced team — is flagged at once with what to fix. In fixed-team tournaments, the "already played against" limit names the teams.

#### Fewer unread match notifications

**For players** · "Added you to a match" is now marked as read once you have opened the match or seen its MMR result. Notifications asking you to act, like validating a score, stay unread until you do.

#### "My profile" in every tournament, team stats for fixed teams

**For players** · The "My profile" stats tab, once for ranked seasons only, now shows your record, form and results in every tournament. Badges appear only where the tournament offers them, and fixed-team tournaments rank teams instead of individual teammates.

#### A tournament that has not started says when it starts

Before its start date, standings and stats show when it runs and a button to join, instead of an empty page. Matches can still be planned ahead.

#### Other changes

- Match lists show each fixed team's name above its players
- On a phone, fixed teams are picked from a full-screen list with search, like players
- Notification cards now look like the rest of the app's cards

### 🐛 Fixed

#### Entering a fixed-team match result

When picking the winner, each side now shows the team's name and players instead of an empty "Team 1" / "Team 2". Tapping a score field selects it, so typing 8 gives 8.

#### Ranked season changes that were not saved

**For super admins** · Changing a ranked season's team sizes or organisation, or clearing its description, showed a success message but changed nothing. It is now saved, even once the season has started; matches already played keep their teams.

#### Creating an account signs you in right away

After creating an account with an email and a password, you now land on the app already
signed in. Before, you were sent back to the login screen and had to reload the page.

#### Signing up with single sign-on now uses your invitation code

Creating your account through your organisation's single sign-on after entering an
invitation code now takes you straight in. Before, the app asked for the code a second time.

#### Fixed-team leagues no longer stop after a few matches

The "max with same partner" limit could block a fixed team after its first matches. It now only applies when teams change from one match to the next, and is greyed out for fixed teams in the tournament settings.

#### Other changes

- **For super admins** · No more stray "(v)" after the team mode in the admin tournament list
- **For tournament admins and super admins** · A disputed match now notifies every organiser of the tournament and every super admin
- Closing a tournament on one device or tab no longer stops live updates on the others
- A match confirmed by both opponents at the same moment is no longer validated twice
- **For tournament admins and super admins** · A second confirmation on the same administration page now works without reloading
