Two optional votes an organizer switches on per championship or ranked season. They reward
the way people play, never the result: no vote ever moves the standings or a rating.

### Fair-play

Right after a match, a small prompt offers a 👍 for each opponent in two taps — ignore it
and it goes away. A 👎 takes a reason (bad faith, attitude, lateness…) and stays anonymous.
Organizers get an alert when a player collects too many in a month, and only they can see
who cast them.

### MVP

In team matches, every player names the best player on the table, teammate or opponent.
The match MVP earns a badge on the match card, and members who watched the game can vote
too.

### Rankings worth showing off

The stats tab ranks the most fair players and the MVP leaders, and crowns a fair-play
player and an MVP of the week. Votes stay open for 48 hours, and the season Rewind hands
out awards at the end.
