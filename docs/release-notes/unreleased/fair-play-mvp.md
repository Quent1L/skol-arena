title: Fair-play and MVP votes
---
Organisers can switch on two optional votes for a tournament or ranked season. After a match, players give their opponents a 👍 for fair-play — or a 👎 with a reason, from the match page — and, in team matches, pick the MVP, which spectators can vote for too. Votes stay open 48 hours and never affect MMR or standings.

A prompt right after the match offers the vote in two taps. The stats tab ranks fair-play and MVP over 30 days (positive figures only), your own figures sit privately in "My profile", and ranked seasons show the fair-play player and MVP of the week. Organisers get a fair-play page to spot repeated issues, and the season recap gains a fair-play and MVP card.
