title: Fair-play and MVP votes
---
Organizers can now switch on two optional votes for their tournament or ranked season.
After a match, players can give their opponents a 👍 for great fair-play — or a 👎,
with a reason, from the match page — and, in team matches, pick the match MVP.
Members who watched a match can vote for the MVP too. Votes stay open for 48 hours and
never change the MMR or the standings.

Right after a match, a small prompt offers the vote in two taps; ignore it and it goes
away. The stats tab shows the fair-play ranking of the last 30 days and the MVP ranking —
positive figures only — and your own figures, visible to you alone. In ranked
seasons, the fair-play player of the week sits in the "This week" card. Organizers get a fair-play page to spot
repeated issues, and the season recap gains a fair-play and MVP card.
