type: improved
title: A smoother app, on a phone too
---
Every screen of the app has been rebuilt on new foundations. Things look the way they did,
but menus, lists and dialogs now behave the same everywhere and fit a phone. The help
bubbles next to statistics open with a tap, and choosing a custom match date offers a
calendar.

**For super admins**: the administration now works on a phone too, and a menu lists every
section, so moving from one to another no longer means going back to the dashboard.
