type: new
title: See how a tournament is played
---
A new "Format" card on a tournament's info tab sums up the settings that matter to players, in plain words: the type of competition, the match format (1 vs 1, teams formed freely or fixed teams), draws and scores, points per result, match limits, how results are validated, and the after-match votes. In ranked seasons, the explanation of how MMR works now lives there too. The written rules keep their own card, now called "Rulebook".
