---
type: fixed
title: Lists and forms that misbehaved
---
- Drop-down lists, such as the language choice in the settings or the tournament filter on
  a profile, sometimes opened far from the field they belong to. They now open right under it.
- On a phone, the teams page cut off the "Create" button and squeezed team names: actions
  now sit under the team.
- Submitting the sign-in or password forms with an empty field showed an unreadable error.
  It now says which field is missing.
