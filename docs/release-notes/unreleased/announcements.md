---
type: new
title: Send an announcement to your players
audience: tournament-admins, super-admins
---
From a tournament or ranked season's menu, under "Announcements", write to all its players: new badges, a rule change, a reminder. Super admins can write to every player of the app.

It arrives as a notification, a pop-up shown once on their next visit, or both, with an optional end date for the pop-up. Closing the pop-up marks the notification as read.
