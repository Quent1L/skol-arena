---
type: new
title: Send an announcement to your players
audience: tournament-admins, super-admins
---
Organisers can now write an announcement for every player of their tournament or ranked
season from its menu, under "Announcements": new badges, a rule change, a reminder. Super admins
can also address every player of the app from the administration.

Choose how it reaches them: in their notifications, as a pop-up shown once the next time they
open the tournament (or the app, for an announcement to everyone), or both. Once a player
closes the pop-up, the notification is marked as read too. You can also set an end date:
after that day, the pop-up no longer shows.
