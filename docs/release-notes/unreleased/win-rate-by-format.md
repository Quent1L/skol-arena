title: See your win rate in 1v1, 2v2 and every other format
---
Your ranked profile now shows your win rate for each format you have played — 1v1, 2v2,
3v3, and uneven matches like 2v1 — with your wins and losses in each. No need to make the
top five of the season statistics to know where you stand. It also appears on player
pages.
