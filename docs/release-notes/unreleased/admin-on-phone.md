type: improved
title: The administration works on a phone
audience: super-admins
---
Every administration screen now fits a phone: lists show what matters first and gather
their actions behind a single button, forms stack their fields, and dialogs fit the screen.

A menu now lists every administration section. It sits on the side on a large screen and
opens from the button at the top of the page on a phone. Moving from one section to another
no longer means going back to the dashboard.
