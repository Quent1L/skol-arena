type: improved
title: "My profile" in every tournament, team stats for fixed teams
audience: players
---
The "My profile" tab of a tournament's stats is no longer reserved for ranked seasons: every tournament you play in now shows your record, recent form and results there, and your fair-play and MVP figures have moved into it. The badges section only appears when the tournament offers badges. In tournaments with fixed teams, the stats now rank teams rather than listing each teammate separately.
