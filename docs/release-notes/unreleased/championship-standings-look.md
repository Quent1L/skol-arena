type: improved
title: Championship standings now look like ranked leaderboards
---
Championship standings use the same cards as ranked seasons: your rank, points and record
at a glance, with your own row highlighted. On a phone, drag between the official and
provisional standings, just like on a ranked season. On a large screen the tiebreakers stay
in columns; on a phone, tap the question mark on a row to see how ties are broken.
