type: new
title: Matches with three sides or more
---
A match can now oppose more than two sides: a 1v1v1 kart race, a 2v2v2 pétanque game, up to
eight sides. Organisers choose the maximum number of sides per match in the championship or
ranked season settings; it stays at two by default.

When entering such a match, add the sides you need, then put them in finishing order — or let
the scores do it. Ties are possible. Championships can award points by result, by a table of
points per place, or by score, and ranked seasons adjust everyone's rating from where they
finished.
