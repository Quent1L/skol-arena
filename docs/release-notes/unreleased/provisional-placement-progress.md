type: fixed
title: The live ranking now counts matches awaiting validation in placement progress
audience: players
---
In the "Provisional (Live)" view of a ranked season, a match not yet validated already
moved your MMR and your recent form, but not your placement progress: you could stay
"3/5 placement matches" after playing your fourth. It now counts, and finishing your
placements with such a match places you in the live ranking straight away.
