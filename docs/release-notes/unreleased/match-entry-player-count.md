---
type: improved
title: Clearer player count when entering a match
---
When you enter a match, the tournament's format now shows right under "Participants" (for example "1 vs 1 · 2 players"), and picking one player too many is flagged at once, before you can go on. The message says what to fix: "This tournament is played 1 vs 1: select exactly 2 players". When splitting players into teams, a side with too many or too few players is flagged too.

In fixed-team tournaments, the "already played against" limit now names the teams instead of listing their players.
