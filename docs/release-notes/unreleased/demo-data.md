title: Try Skol Arena with ready-made demo data
audience: self-hosters
---
Start a new instance with `INIT_DEMO_DATA=true` and it comes filled with a pool club that has been playing for a few months: sixteen players, leagues, a ranked season, a cup bracket, teams and close to 400 matches — some waiting for your confirmation, one disputed. Fair-play and MVP votes are already cast, so their rankings are filled in. The login page shows the admin's email and password, with a button that signs you in in one click; sign in as any player to see every screen in use. A ready-made `docker-compose.demo.yml` is in the repository; see [Demo data](/docs/deployment#demo-data).
